About Me

I am a Japanese-American artist currently living in San Jose, CA. Throughout my life I have moved to two different countries and 5 different states. As a result of my frequent moving, I have learned a lot about how it feels living in completely different environments and the feeling of loss for a place. This is the motivation behind my work and why I create. I gather inspiration from my past and the people around me to create my recent work. I am a multimedia digital artist that utilizes Blender, Adobe Photoshop, Premier Pro, Illustrator, After Effects, Touch Designer and Logic Pro X. Using these programs I share my perception and understanding of the world through my artistic voice.

PROGRAMS — TouchDesigner, Blender, Adobe Photoshop, Premier Pro, Illustrator, After Effects, Logic Pro X
INTERESTS — Storytelling, interactive stories, animation
